City Park Waves Activation Rules

The goal is simple: get outside, operate from a real public park, and make enough valid contacts to count the activation.

Your QSO requirement is tied automatically to the FCC license class saved with your verified callsign account.

📡 QSO Requirements

License Class Minimum QSOs
Technician 5
General 10
Amateur Extra 10

✅ Verified Operator

Sign in before submitting an activation. City Park Waves reads the station/operator callsign from your ADIF log and verifies that it is an active amateur radio callsign.

If your callsign is not active and verified, you cannot submit a City Park Waves activation.

The site's QSO requirement comes from the FCC license class returned when the callsign in the uploaded log is verified.

🌳 Operate From the Park

Your station must be physically located within the boundaries of the selected public park during the activation.

The activation is tied to one City Park Waves park reference.

📝 Valid Log

Submit an ADIF log containing valid QSO records. Each contact must include a contacted callsign, and the log should include date/time information.

The station callsign in the log must match the callsign tied to your verified account.

🎙️ Modes & Bands

Any legal amateur-radio mode or band may be used: SSB, CW, FT8, RTTY, digital, satellites, and more.

There is no power-level or equipment requirement.

🚫 Duplicate Submissions

The same activation log may only be submitted once for a park. City Park Waves creates a fingerprint from the QSO data and blocks duplicate submissions automatically.

City Park Waves is intended to encourage portable operating, not make it complicated. If the operator is really in the park, uses their verified callsign, and meets the required QSO count, the activation counts.

City Park Waves Achievements

Achievements are earned automatically from valid City Park Waves activation logs. There is nothing to claim manually — once your logged activity reaches a milestone, the badge appears on your public operator profile.

🚩 First Activation: A park's first activation goes to the operator with the earliest recorded valid activation for that park.
