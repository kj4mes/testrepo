How City Park Waves Works

🌳 1. Find a Park

Discover nearby city and community parks suitable for portable amateur radio.

📅 2. Plan an Activation

Announce where and when you'll be operating so other hams know to listen for you.

📡 3. Get on the Air

Set up your portable station and call CQ from the park.

🤝 4. Make Contacts

Log your contacts and help put local parks on the air.

Every Park Can Make Waves.

City Park Waves is about getting amateur radio operators outdoors and putting local parks on the air — from neighborhood parks to downtown green spaces that aren't part of traditional park activation programs.
